          FAQ

          Questions owners ask us.

          Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude ourselves?

          For one-off tasks, you should. A worker runs every day without being asked, works inside your systems, follows your rules, hands over to people when it should, and is tested and monitored. Setting that up and keeping it working is our job.

          What if the worker makes a mistake?

          Risky actions need your approval. Any mistake shows up on the timesheet. We fix the cause, add the case to the worker's tests and re-test before the fix goes live.

          What does it cost?

          A fixed price for the pilot, quoted after the first call, then a monthly fee for each live worker. For high-volume jobs, part of the fee can be tied to results.

          How long until we see results?

          About four weeks to a worker tested on your own cases. Lead replies show the difference within days of going live. Collections take a full payment cycle to measure.

          Does it replace our team?

          No. It takes the repetitive part of the job off their plate and hands them the cases that need a person, with the full context attached.

          What if the pilot shows it won't work?

          You'll know before anything goes live, because the trial runs on your past cases. You keep the test report, which shows exactly where the job is hard to automate.

          Do we need technical staff?

          No. We need a few hours from someone who knows the job well, during the first week and during testing.

          Which AI models do you use?

          Whichever performs best on your tests, usually from Anthropic or OpenAI, under business terms. Because every worker is tested, we can change the model without changing how it behaves for you.

          Do you work outside India?

          Yes. We work remotely with teams anywhere.
