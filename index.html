Questions people ask.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude ourselves?

For one-off tasks, you should. A worker runs every day without being asked, inside your systems and by your rules, and it is tested and monitored. Setting that up and keeping it working is our job.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky waits for your approval, and every action is logged. We fix the cause, add the case to the worker's tests and re-test before the fix goes live.

Does it replace our team?

No. It takes the repetitive part of the job off their plate and hands them the cases that need a person, with the context attached.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We need a few hours from someone who knows the job well, in the first week and during the trial.

We're building our own agents. Can you help?

Yes. We build test suites from your real cases, compare models on quality, cost and speed, and rescue pilots that stalled after the demo.

Do you work outside India?

Yes, with teams anywhere, remotely.
